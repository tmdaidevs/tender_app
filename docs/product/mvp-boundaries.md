# MVP boundaries

TenderLoop is an AI-native procurement marketplace for DACH digital and
professional services. It aggregates public demand, builds evidence-backed
supplier profiles, enables structured private RFQs, matches qualified
suppliers, hosts bids confidentially until the deadline, and supports
criteria-based human evaluation and awards.

The launch wedge covers software, cloud, Data & AI, cybersecurity, marketing,
design, consulting, training, and managed services, generally below €250,000.

Included: official-source tender attribution, verified Company Bid Profiles,
deterministic eligibility, explainable Fit Scores, private and invite-only
tenders, buyer-approved AI structuring, matching, invitations, clarifications,
addenda, immutable versions, structured comparison, explicit human awards,
audit history, analytics, and subscription scaffolding.

Excluded: submission to public procurement portals, claims of cryptographic
bid sealing, payments and success fees, autonomous awards or signatures,
autonomous production changes or external communications, and
competitor-derived price recommendations.

North-star metric: percentage of native private tenders receiving at least
three qualified bids within seven days.
